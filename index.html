Fruit & plant information

Fruits and ingredients featured in the presentation

These are the benefit descriptions presented in the supplied material, summarized without adding unlisted medical claims.

🍑

White Peach

Described as nutrient-rich and a source of vitamins and antioxidants; the presentation lists digestion, heart health, skin protection, allergy symptoms and gut disorders.

❤️

Pomegranate

Listed for nutrients, antioxidants, inflammation, heart and urinary health, antimicrobial properties, exercise endurance, brain and digestive health, libido and blood pressure.

🫐

Mixed Berries

Described as antioxidant-rich and high in fibre; the presentation lists blood-sugar and insulin response, inflammation, cholesterol, skin, cancer protection and artery health.

🫒

Olive Fruit Extract

The presentation specifies olive fruit—not olive leaf—with natural hydroxytyrosol, and lists heart, blood-flow, joint, inflammation, oxidation and skin-care benefits.

🌸

Sakura

Described as containing bioactive compounds for skin; the presentation lists skin repair, barrier support, brightening, inflammation, signs of ageing, hyperpigmentation and regeneration.

🌿

Ceramosides

Listed for skin-cell health, moisture-barrier support, helping prevent dryness and irritation, environmental protection and protection against some germs.

🟠

Curcumin

The presentation describes curcumin as a bioactive natural anti-inflammatory compound, antioxidant support, brain-derived neurotrophic factor support, and possible heart-health benefits.

🍃

Gymnema Sylvestre

A climbing shrub native to tropical forests of India, Africa and Australia; the presentation lists sugar-craving, blood-sugar, insulin, cholesterol, triglyceride, weight and inflammation benefits.
