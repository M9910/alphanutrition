HEART • CARDIOVASCULAR • BLOOD SUGAR

Heart Health & Healthy Nutrition

Learn how everyday food choices can support cardiovascular health, healthy blood sugar levels and a lower risk of heart disease and type 2 diabetes.

Protect Your Heart Through Everyday Choices ❤️

A heart-healthy eating pattern can help support healthy blood pressure, cholesterol and blood sugar. It can also help lower cardiovascular disease risk when combined with physical activity, healthy weight management, not smoking and appropriate medical care.

❤️ CardiovascularChoose more vegetables, fruits and whole grains.
🩸 Blood SugarLimit added sugars and highly refined foods.
🫀 Heart DiseaseFavor fiber-rich and minimally processed foods.
🌿 DiabetesHealthy eating patterns can help manage blood sugar and reduce risk.

Can food prevent a heart attack?

No single food can guarantee prevention of a heart attack. However, a consistent heart-healthy eating pattern can help reduce important risk factors. The focus is on the overall pattern: plenty of vegetables and fruits, whole grains, legumes, nuts and other nutrient-rich foods, while limiting excess sodium, added sugars and saturated/trans fats.

If you have chest pain, pressure, shortness of breath, fainting or other possible heart-attack symptoms, seek emergency medical care immediately. Nutrition is supportive care—not an emergency treatment.

Foods to Support Heart Health

These foods can be part of a balanced eating pattern. They are not medicines and no single food can prevent a heart attack on its own.

🥕

Carrots

Rich in fiber and plant nutrients. Add them to meals, salads or soups as part of a varied diet.

🫜

Beetroot

A colorful vegetable that can fit into a heart-healthy pattern alongside other vegetables.

🍎

Apples

A convenient fruit that provides fiber and can replace highly processed or sugary snack choices.

🫚

Ginger

Useful for flavoring meals and drinks, helping you build variety without relying on excess salt.

🍋

Lemon

Add fresh lemon to meals and water for flavor, making it easier to choose low-sugar drinks.

🌿

Celery

A crunchy vegetable that can contribute to a varied intake of vegetables and fiber.

🥬

Spinach

A leafy green vegetable that fits well into heart-healthy meals and balanced diabetes-friendly patterns.

🌾

Whole Grains

Oats, brown rice and other whole grains provide fiber and can support heart and metabolic health.
